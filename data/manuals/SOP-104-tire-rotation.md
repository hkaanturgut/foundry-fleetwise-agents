# SOP-104: Tire Rotation

Applies to: LightDuty vehicles. Required skill: Tires.

## Interval
Every 10,000 km or 180 days.

## Procedure
1. Rotate front-to-rear on the same side for directional tires; cross pattern for non-directional.
2. Minimum tread depth: 3 mm. Below the minimum, replace the tire instead of rotating.
3. Torque lug nuts to specification and re-check after 100 km.
