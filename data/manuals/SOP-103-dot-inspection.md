# SOP-103: Annual DOT Inspection

Applies to: HeavyDuty vehicles only. Required skill: DotInspector (certified inspectors only).

## Interval
Every 365 days. Distance does not change this interval.

## Rules
1. Only a certified DotInspector may sign the inspection. No other technician may perform it.
2. If no certified inspector is available in the customer's team, the work order must stay unassigned and be escalated to the fleet manager; never borrow an inspector from another customer.
3. A vehicle past its DOT inspection date must not be dispatched on public roads.
