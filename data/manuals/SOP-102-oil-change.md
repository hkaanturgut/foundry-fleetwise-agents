# SOP-102: Oil and Filter Change

Required skill: Mechanic (LightDuty) or DieselMechanic (HeavyDuty).

## Interval
- LightDuty: every 8,000 km or 180 days.
- HeavyDuty: every 15,000 km or 90 days.

## Procedure
1. Drain oil at operating temperature; replace the drain plug washer.
2. Replace the oil filter; HeavyDuty also replaces the fuel water separator.
3. Refill to the manufacturer's specification and record the odometer reading.
4. Note leaks or unusual metal in the oil in the work order notes.
