# SOP-101: Brake Inspection

Applies to: all LightDuty and HeavyDuty vehicles. Required skill: Brakes.

## Interval
- LightDuty: every 20,000 km or 365 days, whichever comes first.
- HeavyDuty: every 25,000 km or 180 days, whichever comes first.

## Procedure
1. Measure front and rear pad thickness on both sides.
2. Minimum pad thickness: 4 mm for LightDuty, 6 mm for HeavyDuty. Below the minimum, replace pads on the whole axle.
3. Inspect rotors for scoring deeper than 1.5 mm.
4. Check brake fluid level and moisture; replace fluid above 3% moisture.
5. HeavyDuty only: measure air brake pushrod stroke; out-of-adjustment brakes take the vehicle out of service.

## Escalation
Any out-of-service finding must be reported to the fleet manager the same day (see SOP-500).
